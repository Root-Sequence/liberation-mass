## In one sentence

Liberation Mass is an open, remixable gathering practice for solidarity, healing, shared meaning, mutual aid, and collective action without permanent leadership or required doctrine.

## If you remember only three things

1. It has two linked modes: **Commons** for testimony and coordination, and **Rite** for art, ritual, rest, connection, and renewal.
2. Roles share work, not power; stewardship rotates.
3. Consent, access, exit, safety, and aftercare are core infrastructure—not optional polish.

## Current reality

This repository defines a living experimental format, principles, roles, spaces, agendas, modules, and facilitation resources. It is not a church, cult, finished institution, universal ritual, or software platform. Local groups may adapt it, but remixability does not excuse unsafe practice or erase the people affected by decisions.

## Where it fits

Liberation Mass is the ecosystem’s embodied-practice branch. It gives abstract ideas—care, access, anti-hierarchy, stewardship, shared meaning, mutual aid—a place to be practiced by actual people. Community Infrastructure may learn from gathering needs, but neither project depends on or governs the other.
